Onsets come from spectral flux — energy that has just appeared — and the beat is a phase-locked loop nudged by each onset rather than reset by it, so one stray transient cannot throw the grid. Flux is bass-weighted, because the kick is what carries the pulse people move to; measuring across the whole spectrum lets hats and cymbals, which subdivide, outvote it.

It will not choose an octave for you. Real music often has no single right answer: the 1991 remix in this repo has its strongest periodicity at 116, which is the dotted pulse of its own 174, while an offline pass over the whole track prefers 88. All three are genuinely present, so “also heard at” shows the rivals and the tempo button halves or doubles what he dances on.
