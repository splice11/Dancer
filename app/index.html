Every bone angle is a damped spring chasing a target. Distal bones are softer, so the elbow leads and the hand trails. A dance move is therefore just a target pose set on the beat — the springs generate everything between. Looseness scales the damping: below 1 he goes robotic, above it he goes rubbery.
