Dancer — rig test

He idles on his own, and he can see you. Drag the stage to walk around him; switch on watch cursor and he will follow it with his eyes, turning his head first and his body after.

pose editor →

Every bone angle is a damped spring chasing a target. Distal bones are softer, so the elbow leads and the hand trails. A dance move is therefore just a target pose set on the beat — the springs generate everything between. Looseness scales the damping: below 1 he goes robotic, above it he goes rubbery.

The skeleton is 3D; the renderer is not. Joints solve in his own space, turn, project to the screen, then draw as the same flat capsules. That works because the art style has no surface to get wrong — a black capsule looks identical from every angle. His eyes are two discs riding the head sphere, so they foreshorten and slip round the side as he turns.
